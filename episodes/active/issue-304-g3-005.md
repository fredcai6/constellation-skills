### assertion:issue-304-g3-005.a3
- kind: observed-behavior
- strength: strong
- lifecycle-standing: active
- statement: NOT DETERMINABLE AT THIS GATE, and recorded as the measurement gap the pre-registration at 1662b90 asked for rather than scored either way. The reason is concrete: this gate ran a single scripted drive of the engine BY THE AGENT WHO AUTHORED THE ANCHOR, which is not a behavioural sample of tool ordering and cannot yield a map_before_src figure comparable to PRE-B's five runs. What the gate did establish, by command: `grep -n 'map_before_src' scripts/map_orient.py` returns NO MATCH (exit 1) -- the gate reads the receipt's CONTENT (substitutes, unmapped, escalation) and observes nothing about when any file was read, and the emitted receipt (map-orientation.json) records emitted_at, candidates and hashes with no record of any source read. So a run can still read fifty source files, then orient, then advance context, and be exactly compliant: the measured defect -- lateness -- remains unenforced. Against that, the engine DID refuse the context advance outright until a receipt was discharged, and context precedes understand and plan, so the anchor now hard-gates an early step rather than merely exhorting. Those observations support 'insufficient' and do NOT rule out 'irrelevant', because separating the two requires runs under BOTH anchors with map_before_src measured identically -- a comparative experiment nobody has run.
