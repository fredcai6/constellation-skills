# Operative packets and authority

A handoff is a targeted packet. It states directly the operative task, applicable scope, permissions, constraints, checks, stop conditions and return contract. Read it first, local project guidance and the selected Required Inputs. Required Inputs supply evidence needed for this slice. Provenance supplies optional audit/conflict context, never automatic transitive reading or authority; open it for real ambiguity, conflict or missing evidence and record why. Distill source meaning faithfully into the packet without copying long sources or dropping applicable constraints.

The Reviewer assesses packet scope, sufficiency, fidelity and relevant omissions against selected source evidence as well as reviewing the implementation. Judge meaning rather than particular wording: an equivalent concise formulation is sufficient. A missing material constraint or permission not authorized by evidence is a finding.

Neither artifact authoring nor its provenance authorizes future implementation. An approved implementation plan authorizes its approved bounded actions; explicit scoped direction by a human or higher-level agent can authorize work or a recorded override. Proceed within that scope without demanding a redundant checkpoint solely because it was waived under explicit authority. Do not invent acceptance, broaden authority or remove required evidence. Report genuine gaps and conflicts to the directing principal.

Use the named result artifact and role template, with the exact assigned gate and lowercase Return status. Keep routine checks concise with evidence pointers; retain trigger, evidence, impact and resolution criteria for findings. Preserve prior results and stable finding IDs through repair and independent re-review. A complete implementation is a claim for independent review; review disposition is not human acceptance. APPROVE may carry nonblocking observations. BLOCK identifies material defects or missing required meaning/evidence/authority; advisory or inconclusive reviews may use COMMENT where the delivery contract supports it. Return status describes completion: a finished BLOCK or COMMENT is complete, while partial/blocked means the review could not finish.
