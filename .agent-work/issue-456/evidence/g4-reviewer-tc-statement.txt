tc(new): g4's two-tier routing claim does not hold uniformly across corpus shapes. Confirmed
on a REAL corpus already in hand (this repo's own map/INDEX.md, fresh build) and on two
synthetic shapes: a flat dominant package (this repo's own tests/, 50 modules / 2769 entities =
74.3% of the corpus, zero tier-2 subheadings -- identical to Shape A, a synthetic single-package
30-module flat tree) and a loose-top-level-module corpus with no packages at all (Shape B,
synthetic), where tier 1 ITSELF degenerates to a one-package-per-module list, falsifying
top_index's own docstring claim that tier 1 is "bounded by how many top-level packages the
corpus has, never by how many modules." Both shapes are real-world, not pathological (default
pytest flat-test layout; small script-bag repos with no package structure). Not a blocker for
g4 -- the tier stays derived, not tuned, and reports these shapes honestly rather than
fabricating structure -- but the top_index docstring's tier-1 bound claim should be corrected or
scoped, and a later gate should decide whether a third grouping signal (e.g. filename-prefix
clustering for flat packages) is worth adding. Evidence:

g4-reviewer-r1-finding.txt.
