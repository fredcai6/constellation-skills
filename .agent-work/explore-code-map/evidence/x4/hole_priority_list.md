## How this is ordered, and why not by PageRank

(`docs/architecture/`) chose to name. **PageRank did not beat a random draw** at any K
(p >= 0.17; top-10 lift 0.87x). It promotes widely-called private micro-helpers —
50% of its top 30 are underscore-prefixed — which is exactly the class of
entity an architecture map deliberately omits.

**In-weight (total internal call count) did validate**: 60% of its top 10 and 47% of its
top 30 are entities the curated map names, against a 19.4% base rate (p=0.005 and
p=0.0005). So this list is ordered by in-weight over public entities, and carries the
PageRank rank as a column for comparison rather than as the ordering.

Treat it as a prioritized worklist, not an oracle: a validated 2.4x lift still means
roughly half the top entries are things the map felt no need to name.
