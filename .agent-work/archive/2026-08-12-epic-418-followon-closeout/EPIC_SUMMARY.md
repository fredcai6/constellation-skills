Closed 2026-08-12. Admiral session `717403d3-70be-436f-bc06-ce9ac3e34e05`.

## What the epic was for

Agents kept reaching for the engine CLI instead of the MCP door. The epic's job was to make the door
the whole access path, so an agent never needs to know a CLI exists.

## What shipped

Thirteen workstreams merged.

- **All eighteen engine verbs reach through the door.** N1 closed the last five. No role now carries
  an instruction it cannot execute through the door.
- **A spine is compiled, not hand-written.** A typed spec goes in; the generator refuses to emit
  anything the validating oracle would reject. Authors stopped writing checks as shell strings.
- **A crew that cannot satisfy a check blocks to its recorded parent** rather than stalling or
  guessing.
- **The work lifecycle became one thing.** One call opens branch, worktree, work area and spine
  together; the closing advance archives the work area spine-last; a gate's dispatch is declared in
  the spec and checked against the registry.
