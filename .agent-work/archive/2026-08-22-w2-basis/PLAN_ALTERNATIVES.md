# Plan Alternatives Brief — w2-basis

## The one thing being designed twice
What counts as a "basis" for a `check: null` postcondition — authored at plan time, rendered by the engine, required at attest — and which of `COMMANDER_SPINE.template.json`'s real 19 `check: null` conditions (8 preconditions + 11 postconditions) it should actually be attached to.

## Count and panel — a surfaced choice
N=3, panel (not 2 or single-with-untaken-roads). This design touches the engine (`checklist_engine.py`) and is load-bearing corpus-wide infrastructure per `decision:engine-first-backfill-where-it-earns-it` — "when in doubt, panel" applies. Dispatched as three independent, real background agents (Agent tool, no shared context), each authoring one full candidate before any converged.

## The constraints (one per agent)
- **structured-field** — a new `basis` sibling field on `Condition` (locator_kind: file/evidence_ref/state_field/command/abstain); `render_human` and `attest` both get new code.
- **statement-convention** — no schema change; fold a `-- BASIS: <kind>:<locator>` convention into the existing `statement` string; `attest` parses it.
- **artifact-conversion** — no new field, no new check kind, no new attest/render code at all; convert qualifying `check: null` conditions directly to `check: {kind: artifact}` or `{kind: command}`, reusing the engine's existing (already-shipped, already-live) refusal machinery verbatim.

## Compared on
Depth, locality, seam placement, testability — per each candidate file at `.agent-work/w2-basis/plan-candidate-<constraint>.md`.

## Framing block (presented ahead of convergence)
- **Dependencies held fixed for all three**: target only `skills/commander/templates/COMMANDER_SPINE.template.json` (19 conditions) + `checklist_engine.py`; never `generate_spine.py`/`specs/`; ship mechanism + authored basis for ONE template, not all 65 corpus-wide; any refusal that is genuinely new ships report-only with a named promotion trigger.
- **Illustrative sketch (NOT a proposal, carries zero weight)**: "maybe every condition gets a one-line `because` string, required non-empty." This is explicitly the shape the epic's hard constraint (`decision:decorative-basis-is-a-failure`) rules out if left unrendered/unrequired — offered only to prime parallel thinking about why a naive fill-in-the-blank fails.

## Independent convergence — the load-bearing finding
All three candidates, dispatched blind to each other, independently measured the SAME real 19 conditions and landed on the **same structural split**, not three different answers:

| band | count (of 19) | all three candidates' shared finding |
|---|---|---|
| genuine artifact already exists | ~5 | `plan.c1` (mission frame file), `reconcile.c1` (doc/map diff), `archive.c2` (branch pushed), `init.c1` (lease state) — these need only a **check-kind promotion** (`null` → `artifact`/`command`), zero new engine code, reuses the engine's existing refusal (already blocking-live on 5 other conditions in this same file today) |
| genuine artifact exists but the REAL claim is judgment, not existence | **2** | `plan.c4` (plan-alternatives run, converged) and `plan.c5` (cold critic run, triaged) — files exist and are checkable, but "converged to one recommendation" / "triaged by human" is a call no mechanical check can make. **This is the only band where a brand-new field earns anything a promoted check kind can't already give it**, per `structured-field`'s explicit count (9 of its 11 "genuine" locators need no new field at all; only these 2 do) and `artifact-conversion`'s independent finding ("converts (mostly)... the ordering claim stays exactly as unverified as it is today"). |
| structurally-guaranteed by gate order | 8 (all preconditions) | `understand.p1`, `plan.p1`, `execute.p1`(half), `reconcile.p1`, `triage.p1`, `review.p1`, `feedback.p1`, `archive.p1` — a `gated` checklist cannot `start` a gate whose predecessor hasn't `advance`d, so these are already true by construction before attest ever runs. `artifact-conversion`: "manufacturing an evidence_type for 'the prior gate finished' would be evidence theater." Touching these would be decorative in the opposite direction — enforcing an already-enforced fact. |
| genuinely no locator (honest-null) | ~4 | `context.c1`'s "loaded" half, `execute.c1`, `triage.c1`, `archive.c3`, `execute.p1`'s headroom half — "did you understand/judge X" claims with no durable artifact. All three candidates independently warn that forcing these into any locator shape (field, statement, or artifact) produces a **worse** outcome than honest `check: null`: a self-authored evidence blob that *looks* engine-checked but proves nothing more than the bare assertion it replaced — `artifact-conversion` names this precisely: "a decorative artifact condition launders the same bare assertion through a mechanism whose whole point was to stop that." |

**Answer to `decision:locator-definition-is-yours`**, stated with the real numbers: of 19 conditions, at most 11/19 (58%) can express any real locator at all, and of those, 9 need no new mechanism — only 2 need one. The other 8/19 (42%, all but one of them preconditions structurally guaranteed by gate order, plus the honest-null band) resist every locator shape tried and must stay `check: null`, unbasised. This is a measured negative on the majority of the corpus, reported per the launch order's Honest-Null Clause rather than smoothed over — and it directly explains why `decision:engine-first-backfill-where-it-earns-it` says ONE template, not a blanket rollout: even within this one template, most conditions don't want what the epic assumed all 65 wanted.

## Cold critic findings & disposition (post-convergence revision)

A single cold critic (per the panel-vs-single record below) reviewed this document against the real source files and found 6 fix-before-execute issues plus 2 note-only ones (full findings: `.agent-work/w2-basis/PLAN_CRITIC.md`). All 6 are accepted and resolved by **narrowing the shipped design**, not by patching around them:

1. **`init.c1`'s promotion misattributed its source and isn't zero-new-code** (accepted) — `state_field` isn't an existing engine check kind; `command` would need an unwritten, unreviewed script against the bookend gate of every Commander run in the corpus. **Resolution: `init.c1` is dropped from this wave entirely.** Stays `check: null`. Recorded as a triage candidate for a future wave with its own red-proof.
2. **"Not a new refusal surface" conflates mechanism-reuse with behavior-preservation** (accepted) — promoting `plan.c1`/`reconcile.c1`/`archive.c2`/`init.c1` from `check: null` to a blocking check IS a real behavior change for four specific gates that have never refused a bare attest before, regardless of whether the engine already runs that check *kind* elsewhere. No Admiral adjudication is in hand for shipping that blocking live. **Resolution: the entire check-kind-promotion batch is dropped from this wave's shipped diff** — not deferred-with-a-flag, actually not shipped. `decision:widening-live-refusal-report-only`'s default (report-only absent an in-hand adjudication) governs, and an `artifact`-kind check has no report-only mode to fall back to (confirmed: `--report-only` is a `command`/`git-change-policy`-only flag, per finding 5's grep), so there is no safe partial version of this batch to ship this wave.
3. **`reconcile.c1`'s conversion is decorative as specified** (accepted) — `_artifact_match_satisfied` only checks agent-*supplied* payload fields, never independently re-runs `git diff`; an agent could attach a `file-diff` evidence item against any path and pass. This is now moot: `reconcile.c1` was part of the dropped batch (finding 2), so it stays `check: null` regardless.
4. **`plan.c2` is missing from all four bands** (accepted) — it has the identical judgment-vs-existence shape as `plan.c4`/`plan.c5` (own analysis in both `structured-field` and `artifact-conversion` candidates agrees). **Resolution: `plan.c2` joins band 2** — three conditions get the new `basis` field this wave, not two: `plan.c2`, `plan.c4`, `plan.c5`.
5. **The promotion trigger ("10 real runs, zero false-refusals") is not mechanically measurable** (accepted) — nothing persists a queryable record of report-only outcomes. **Resolution: `attest()`'s new basis-resolution guard always attaches a durable `basis-check` evidence item** (pass or fail, regardless of report-only/blocking mode) to the condition it evaluates. The promotion trigger is now literally auditable: grep archived spines / the episode store for `basis-check` evidence and count failures. This also strengthens "required at attest" — something durable is produced on every attest, not just on failure.
6. **The two kept locator kinds (`file`, `evidence_ref`) only verify existence, undercutting the stated reason for building new schema at all** (accepted, and this finding changes the plan's own justification, not just its wording) — an existence-only locator gives `plan.c2`/`c4`/`c5` *no more expressive power* than simply promoting them to `check: {kind: artifact}` directly. But `artifact` checks are unconditionally blocking (finding 5's confirmed precedent: no report-only mode exists for that check kind) — so promoting these three to `artifact` this wave would require exactly the un-adjudicated blocking-live behavior change finding 2 just ruled out. **The new `basis` field's actual, honest justification is therefore ROLLOUT SAFETY, not expressiveness**: it is the only mechanism that lets these three conditions move from "bare assertion, no evidence required" to "a real artifact is required to exist, checked and recorded" WITHOUT immediately blocking every Commander run that touches `plan.c2`/`c4`/`c5` before the check has been exercised for real. This is a materially more honest framing than the original "judgment vs. existence" claim, and it is written into the plan below as such.

Findings 7 (`.agent-work/templates/` overlay sync dropped) and 8 (no red-proof named) are accepted as note-only and folded directly into `execute.json`'s gates below, not into this document.

## Output — recommendation (revised after cold critic)

**Winner (revised post-critique): the `basis` field alone — narrowed to `file`/`evidence_ref` locator kinds, report-only, authored on exactly three conditions (`plan.c2`, `plan.c4`, `plan.c5`). No check-kind promotions ship this wave.**

The original hybrid's check-kind-promotion half (band 1: `plan.c1`, `reconcile.c1`, `archive.c2`, `init.c1`) is dropped in full — see "Cold critic findings & disposition" above. It remains real, well-evidenced work (`artifact-conversion`'s own analysis is sound for `plan.c1`/`archive.c2` specifically), preserved as a **triage candidate** for a follow-up wave once an Admiral adjudication for blocking-live is in hand, or once `command`/`artifact` checks grow a report-only mode. Shipping it this wave would mean an un-adjudicated live behavior change on the bookend gate of every Commander run in the corpus (`init.c1`) plus a decorative conversion (`reconcile.c1`) — real risk for a batch that was never the epic's core ask.

`structured-field`'s other three locator kinds (`state_field`, `command`) and `statement-convention` in full lose for the reasons in the original comparison below (unearned generality; text-parsing is more gameable than a structured field for no compensating benefit).

**The design, concretely:**
1. **Add a new `basis` field**, sibling of `check` on a `Condition` — `{"locator_kind": "file"|"evidence_ref"|"abstain", "locator": {...}, "because": "<optional one-line rationale>"}`. Two expressive kinds only (`file`, `evidence_ref`); `state_field` and `command` are named untaken roads (real designs in `plan-candidate-structured-field.md` §1, deliberately not built — no payoff for the 3 target conditions, real added engine-risk).
2. **Author `basis` on exactly three conditions**: `plan.c2` (`file`, `.agent-work/<work-id>/execute.json`), `plan.c4` (`file`, glob `plan-candidate-*.md` min 2 + `PLAN_ALTERNATIVES.md`), `plan.c5` (`file`, `PLAN_CRITIC.md`). All three share the same judgment-vs-existence shape: a real artifact exists and is checkable, but the substantive claim (fidelity, convergence, triage) is a judgment call no mechanical check can fully make.
3. **`render_human` renders the basis line only when populated** — same populated-only convention `constraints`/`anchors`/`directives` already use; zero change to any condition that doesn't carry a `basis`.
4. **`attest`'s new guard**, on a `check: null` condition carrying a populated `basis` (not `abstain`): resolves the locator, and **always attaches a durable `basis-check` evidence item** recording the outcome (resolved / unresolved, locator, path checked) regardless of pass/fail. On failure: **reports (attaches evidence, proceeds) rather than blocks** — this is genuinely new attest code with no live precedent, so `decision:widening-live-refusal-report-only` governs it and its default (report-only absent an in-hand adjudication) applies. **Named promotion trigger**: an Admiral audit, at a named future wave boundary, greps archived spines / the episode store for `basis-check` evidence across N real runs and either rules the false-refusal rate acceptable (promote to blocking) or doesn't (stays report-only, or the locator design is revised). This is now mechanically countable (finding 5's fix), unlike the original numeric-but-unpersisted trigger.
5. **The honest justification for building new schema at all, restated per finding 6**: `file`/`evidence_ref` locators verify existence only — the *same* power a direct `check: {kind: artifact}` promotion would give these three conditions. The `basis` field's real, sole value this wave is **rollout safety**: it is the only mechanism that can move a condition from "bare assertion, zero evidence" toward "a real artifact is required and durably recorded" without immediately blocking every Commander run before the check has been exercised for real — because `artifact`-kind checks have no report-only mode to fall back to. This is a narrower claim than the original plan made, and it is the honest one.
6. **Leave everything else `check: null`, with no basis field** — the 8 preconditions (gate-order-guaranteed), the 4 genuinely-judgment conditions (`context.c1`'s loaded-half, `execute.c1`, `triage.c1`, `archive.c3`), and the 4-condition check-kind-promotion batch deferred to triage (`plan.c1`, `reconcile.c1`, `archive.c2`, `init.c1`). This is the measured, reported answer for those conditions specifically, not an omission.

## Untaken-road record
- **The check-kind-promotion batch (`plan.c1`, `reconcile.c1`, `archive.c2`, `init.c1`)** — real, designed, evidenced (see `artifact-conversion` candidate and this document's Cold Critic Findings §1-3), deliberately not shipped this wave: no Admiral adjudication in hand for the un-adjudicated live-blocking behavior change it implies, `init.c1`'s promotion needs an unwritten script against the highest-blast-radius gate in the corpus, and `reconcile.c1`'s conversion is decorative as specified. Recorded as a triage candidate (recommend-and-defer) for a follow-up wave.
- **`statement-convention` (whole candidate)** — loses to the hybrid above: no unique capability it offers isn't matched or bettered by `artifact-conversion` (render-cost) or `structured-field` (structured, non-gameable locator). Not carried forward.
- **`structured-field`'s `state_field` and `command` locator kinds** — real, designed (see that candidate file §1), deliberately not shipped this wave: zero of this wave's 2 authored conditions need them, and each adds engine-risk (live state introspection; subprocess execution inside `attest`) disproportionate to proving the mechanism. Revisit if/when a backfill wave (measured against episode-store evidence per `decision:engine-first-backfill-where-it-earns-it`) finds a condition that genuinely needs one.
- **Backfilling basis onto the other 14 of 19 conditions in this same template** — measured and explicitly declined for 12 of them (8 gate-order-guaranteed + 4 honest-null); the remaining 3 "weak/circular" preconditions (`understand.p1`-shaped, restating a prior gate's own status) are folded into the gate-order-guaranteed band, not treated separately.
- **A 3-lens critic panel for the cold-critic step** (vs. a single critic) — not run; this decision is well-scoped and the panel-vs-single choice for the *convergence itself* (this document) already used a 3-candidate panel, which is the higher-leverage place to spend the extra agent.

Panel (N=3) for plan-alternatives, per "load-bearing interface + touches architecture (the engine)" → panel default. Single critic (not a panel) for the cold-critic step below — this is a converged, already-measured, already-narrowed plan (not a first-draft architecture proposal), so one adversarial cold read is proportionate; a 3-lens critic panel is reserved for a design that hasn't already been through independent triangulation, which this one has.
