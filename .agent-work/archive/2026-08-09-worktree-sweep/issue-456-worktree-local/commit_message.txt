fix(code_map): handle UTF-8 BOM and wrap-line docstring rendering

Issue #456 gate g8: two code defects fixed.

Defect 1 - BOM-prefixed files: Files starting with UTF-8 byte-order mark
(EF BB BF) failed to parse via ast.parse, causing them to silently drop out
of the map. Added BOM stripping at two ast.parse call sites in extract.py.
Includes test fixture and test_bom_file_can_be_extracted that verifies
the fix works and regresses without it.

Defect 2 - Wrapped docstring rendering: Inconsistency between summary and
body extraction methods. Summary used splitlines()[0] while body used
split("\n")[1:]. Fixed doc_body_of to use splitlines() consistently.
Includes test_wrapped_docstring_body_uses_splitlines that verifies the
fix handles multi-line docstrings correctly.
