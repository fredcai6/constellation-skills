fix(code_map): preserve docstring overflow when no blank line separator

Issue #456 gate g8 remediation-2: Previous fix silently dropped content
from dense paragraphs (no blank line) exceeding 160 chars. This regressed
the dominant case: 132+ docstrings in scripts/** alone become truncated
with no body, losing content mid-clause.

Fix: When there's no blank line and paragraph exceeds 160 chars, split
at 160 and put overflow into body instead of discarding it. This preserves
all docstring content while maintaining the wrapped-summary-whole behavior.

- _first_paragraph() detects no-blank-line case and handles overflow
- Emit sites still apply [:160] truncation (harmless double-check)
- doc_body_of() retrieves overflow from _first_paragraph()

Added critical boundary test: dense paragraph >160 chars, no blank line.
Test confirms summary truncates AND overflow appears in body (not lost).
Break-test verified: reverting overflow fix makes test go RED (221 chars
instead of truncated 160).

This preserves the design invariant: no docstring content is silently lost.
