fix(code_map): wrapped docstring summaries now span paragraphs, not lines

Issue #456 gate g8 remediation: Defect 2 (D3) properly fixed.

Docstring summaries that wrapped across multiple physical lines were being
cut at the first newline instead of the first paragraph boundary (blank line
per PEP 257). A wrapped summary sentence would be split in half: first
physical line as summary, remainder becoming the body start.

Solution: Factor paragraph extraction into _first_paragraph() helper used
by both summary and body extraction, ensuring they never drift apart.

- doc_summary_of(doc): Extract first paragraph, join lines to single spaces
- doc_body_of(node): Extract text after first blank line (via _first_paragraph)
- Updated three summary emission sites to use helper
- BOM stripping added to SourceScan in checks.py (needed for check validation)

Tests added cover boundary cases:
- Wrapped summary + blank line + body (core defect)
- One-line docstring (summary only, no body)
- Wrapped summary with no blank line (entire docstring is summary)

Break-test confirmed: test_wrapped_summary_is_first_paragraph fails (RED)
when reverting to line-based splitting, passes (GREEN) with fix.

Closing selector: 8 passing for -k 'bom or docstring' (was 6 at start of
this pass, was 4 before first pass).
