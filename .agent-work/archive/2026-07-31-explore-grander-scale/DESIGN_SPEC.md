## Intent

Constellation began as a rigor system for coding agents. This exploration asked whether it should become something larger: a durable coordination and knowledge substrate — shared cognitive infrastructure for Tommy and the agents working with him — in which ideas acquire structure, interact, and become actionable, and in which agents are replaceable workers over durable state rather than carriers of ever-growing instructions.

This is a personal experimentation project. Its value is enabling ideas, learning, and enjoyment; it has no utility-based kill condition and does not need to outperform ordinary notes. Tommy builds it himself as a learning playground. Five purposes anchor it: personal experimentation; honest project maps that serve both agent action and human understanding; a cross-project network where accumulated material forms unexpected ideas; genuine agent autonomy under human primacy; and revision-controllable graph history.

"Done" for this spec is narrower than the vision, and deliberately tests only half of it. This cut builds the **instruction-system half**: a map-first support system whose use is observable (B3), and a reworked lessons framework (B1) in which observation is collated before reaction — exercised by at least one consolidation acting on a collated cluster, not just a full store — deletion is safe, and everything between canonical truth and an agent's active context is mechanical. The **idea-substrate half** — where ideas themselves, not run defects or code facts, acquire structure — is an expected later problem: no near-term element builds it, and the near-term elements are required not to foreclose it. The long-arc stratum is not cut; it orients and constrains what is built now so the near-term work grows toward the substrate rather than away from it.

Scope of this cut, stated plainly: **B1 and B3 are the deliverables.** B2's kernel-plus-fragments break is **conditional** — it proceeds only if the evidence gates in B2 are met. B4 is **not in this cut**; its earning trigger is stated in B4.

## Chosen design

The design is one spec in two strata. **Stratum A is direction: it constrains and orients but is not cut into issues.** **Stratum B is the actionable design: it is what gets cut.** Every Stratum B element must be buildable without committing to any Stratum A implementation, and must not foreclose it — and this is checked, not aspirational: the to-issues cut review includes an explicit per-element foreclosure check against Stratum A.

### Stratum A — the long-arc vision (orienting context; DO NOT CUT into issues)

- **A shared substrate, globally networked, locally structured.** The wider knowledge network has no mandatory spine; bounded subnetworks (project frames, a book, a philosophy region) may declare a local spine, schema, invariants, and completion rules when their purpose earns rigor. Frames are deliberately declared, with explicit overlapping membership — never exclusive containment.
- **One assertion truth model.** All truth claims — code structure, historical fact, personal belief, philosophical argument, cross-project analogy — use the same mechanics: an identified assertion with source, supporting and challenging evidence, and a qualitative weak/medium/strong assessment that allocates trust-but-verify attention and creates no inertia against decisive new evidence. Belief strength and lifecycle standing (disputed, superseded, rejected) remain separate dimensions. This paragraph is the model's one home in this spec; the deferred detailed mechanics live on the ideas board.
- **Maps are a frame-local construct, tuned to the present.** A map is how a frame keeps honest track of existing artifacts that change over time — code in a repo, a book manuscript, anything where "what's where right now" must be explicitly known. Within a mapped frame, the map holds strictly what currently is: navigating it never requires reconstructing history, and history and future possibilities sit outside the map, reachable deliberately, never leaking into ordinary traversal. How that non-map material is organized is deliberately unfixed — separate layers, a combined timeline view of an idea as it was and will be, or something finer-grained. The wider network is not map-shaped: frames organize ideas in whatever ways serve them, and the current-truth invariant binds maps specifically, not the network at large. A map may answer "why must this exist?"; "why is it this way?" is not map material.
- **Confederated agents with home frames.** Constellation's agents-on-agents delegation remains the execution shape. A home frame is an explicit instantiation input supplying starting context, purpose, vocabulary, protocol, tools, and authority — an initialization and jurisdictional anchor, not a position. Agents traverse the shared substrate beyond home to understand and explore.
- **Project truth federated into an idea commons.** Project frames own honest operational maps of what exists; a wider cross-project commons connects them with looser notes, concepts, questions, and proposed relationships — shared graph mechanics, distinct epistemic and mutation rules.
- **Git-native authored truth.** The human-reviewable, diffable representation stays canonical; derived indexes, visualizations, and database projections are reproducible accelerators, never a second truth.
- **Human-directed autonomy.** Human primacy is authority, not a universal approval gate: agents hold genuine delegated room to traverse, propose, verify, and act inside explicit envelopes; intent, values, contested interpretation, and consequential promotion remain deliberately human.

### Stratum B — the near-term actionable design

#### B0. Governing principles (normative; apply to every B element)

1. **Sensors and actuators — the stochastic boundary.** LLMs are sensors: they notice, judge, propose, consolidate. Mechanisms are actuators: they assemble, route, enforce, repeat. Nothing stochastic is asked to produce a predictable result — the spine's founding logic, generalized. Precisely where the boundary sits: stochastic work happens **upstream of canon**, where its output lands as reviewable, attributable change to canonical truth; between canonical truth and an agent's active surface, every transformation is deterministic and attributable. Where a vague request must become an exact anchor, the resolution happens *before* the query: the agent proposes the anchor, the mechanical query runs from it, and both the proposed anchor and the query result are recorded.
2. **Collate before reacting.** A single observation never triggers its own doctrine patch. Observations accumulate in a durable, queryable form; consolidation acts on clusters that rhyme.
3. **The two-bin rule** (canonical statement — other sections reference it, never restate it). For any doctrine item, ask: can we afford to observe this failing once? If yes, it lives as prose and is always eligible for experimental pruning under a tripwire. If no, it is mechanized (validator, hook, gate) and its prose version is deleted because the mechanism owns it. A mechanization always carries a pointer to its motivating rationale and episodes — deleting the prose never deletes the why.
4. **Reviewed always; by human eyes when it counts.** Nothing that changes how anything works lands wholly unreviewed — independent agentic review is the standing floor, not a fallback, and its **depth scales with consequence class**: a tripwired prose deletion gets a light single-reviewer pass so experimentation stays cheap; changes to mechanisms, authority rules, or human-facing contracts get the full cold panel. Slow-changing surfaces are generated ahead of time so a diff exists in git, keeping human review possible at any moment, including retrospectively. Whether Tommy's own eyes land on a change — directly, or mediated through conversation with an orchestrator, which counts as human review — is judgment scaled to consequence, not a universal gate.

#### B1. One observation framework (the lessons rework)

Post-fact lessons from real runs and predictive findings from Curator sweeps are one framework, not two. Both produce **observations** in the same what/why/impact shape; they differ only in collection cost and tense.

- **Episodes are durable and queryable.** Before any diagnosis, an observation preserves two explicitly partitioned field groups. **Mechanically captured** (from engine and harness state, zero agent effort): run/project, role and active spine step, context manifest (what was loaded, at which revision), refusals, reopens, rework counts, failed commands, artifact references. **Agent-supplied** (kept deliberately small): task intent, expected behavior, observed behavior, impact/cost, workaround. Suspected cause and proposed remedy are separate, optional assertions. The episode record must remain expressible as assertions under the Stratum A truth model — it is the first accumulating store this design builds, and it must not become a silo the assertion model is later built *alongside* rather than *over*. The current inbox's evaporate-after-graduation behavior is replaced: the structured episode outlives its consolidation, so rhymes remain findable across runs. The store is not exempt from lifecycle: consolidated, superseded, and stale episodes are retired by the same explicit policy discipline the corpus itself lives under (retired means excluded from ordinary rhyme-search, retained in history).
- **Consolidation acts on clusters.** Finding that episodes rhyme is a sensor job (LLM); the store that makes rhymes findable is mechanical. Consolidation output is one of two change classes: a **mechanization change** (new/changed validator, hook, gate, tool) or an **instruction change** (add, amend, merge, split, retire prose) — the routing between them is the two-bin rule (B0.3). Lessons are evidence and diagnostic input — never themselves the active doctrine.
- **Pre-learning makes deletion safe.** A simplification that removes doctrine files a predictive tripwire lesson naming the possible failure mode and the impact judged acceptable. Observed worse impact is pre-attributed evidence for rework. Additions symmetrically post their expected improvement. This — not a numeric budget — is the standing pressure against instruction growth.
- **Curator owns corpus coherence and the evolution boundary.** Curator measures and mends the corpus mechanically, and may dispatch opinionated subagents with specific adversarial viewpoints to hunt incoherence (the cold-critic-panel pattern pointed at the corpus). All such findings enter the observation store as predictive episodes and become **proposals**; they never directly mutate doctrine. Semantic coherence within a concern stays with that concern, verified through use and through these sweeps.

#### B2. Canonical corpus and active surface (projections)

**This is a deliberate architectural break: from one monolithic skill per role to a slim kernel plus just-in-time fragments — and it is staged, not immediate.** Today each role (Commander, Admiral, Curator, …) carries one large skill document holding everything the role might ever need, and every correction has historically made it larger. The target shape is different: a role keeps only a small always-loaded kernel — how to drive and recover the spine, how authority and human escalation work, how to verify a step's result, and how to find the current step's contract — and everything else becomes skill fragments loaded just in time, when the spine step that needs them becomes active. The spine already knows which job is active, so it is the selector for spine-driven work; frames without spines will need their own deterministic selectors, an expected later problem this design must not foreclose. No semantic routing guesses what to load.

**The break's evidence gates.** The premise ("skills grow without bound and that costs us") and the load-bearing claim ("run artifacts carry accumulated state between steps, so late loading never means lost understanding") are currently asserted, not measured. Therefore: B3 and tripwired deletion run **first**. The break proceeds only if (a) the corpus-size trend from git shows deletion alone is not getting the always-loaded role surface small enough, and (b) a role-competence test shows an agent operating from kernel-plus-fragments-plus-artifacts completes a representative mid-spine step as correctly as one holding the monolith. If deletion alone suffices, the break is not taken — that outcome is success, not failure. If the break proceeds, a generated **human-readable whole-role projection** is a required view: today a person can read a role's full behavior in one document, and that affordance must survive fragmentation.

Beneath that shift, the canonical corpus (skills, doctrine, references, map — Markdown in git) grows freely under accrete-then-consolidate. What an agent loads is a **projection**, and projections are always deterministic:

- **Ahead-of-time generation** for slow-changing, role-shaped doctrine: a versioned script builds the projection, so every doctrine change produces a reviewable diff of what agents will actually see.
- **Per-run assembly** for task working sets: deterministic recipes keyed by the active spine node — the workflow state already knows which job is active, so no semantic router infers anything.
- **A manifest always exists**: whatever was loaded, the record of what and from which revision is a free byproduct of deterministic assembly. This manifest is the episode's context field and the map-use observability instrument — no new watching infrastructure.
- The spine node owns the *what/when* (purpose, inputs, outputs, authority, postconditions); the loaded capability module owns the *how*; the orchestrator kernel owns the human relationship, state transitions, and reopening; run artifacts carry state between nodes.

#### B3. Map-first support system (the tracer)

The high-value tracer: express Commander map-first intake as one canonical concern-owned contract, projected into Commander `context`/`plan`, deleting the scattered prose it supersedes.

- **Name the map-input contract.** Commander's context step gets a resolved canonical entrypoint (e.g. `docs/architecture/index.md` or an explicit absent-state), replacing the current scattered, underbound references.
- **Make map use observable — with an honest instrument.** Two evidence sources, each doing only what it can. The projection **manifest** answers *what was made available, at which revision* — delivery, not use. The *ordering* question — did map orientation precede broad source crawling — is answered from the **run transcript**, which already records tool calls in order as a harness byproduct; extracting map-reads-vs-source-reads ordering from it is analysis of an existing record, not new watching infrastructure. If transcript analysis proves insufficient, building a true access trace is a priced decision, not a silent assumption.
- **Degraded mode is explicit — for what is detectable.** The mechanically detectable trigger is the entrypoint failing to resolve (missing map, dangling canonical reference): that produces a reported degraded mode — slower and visibly less certain work — never a silent fallback to full code crawling. Stale and contradictory maps currently have no detector; building one is an open thread, not a claimed guarantee. What *is* mechanized now: the **drift check** — regenerate the projection from current canon and diff against the committed artifact; a mismatch fails loudly. By the two-bin rule this is catastrophic-class (agents silently loading stale doctrine) and therefore mechanism-owned from day one. The map accelerates cognition; it must not become the only place cognition is possible.
- **Success criterion (human-owned), with an adjudicator and consequences.** Agents use high-level architecture connections to orient before inspecting targeted code, with code as targeted verification of a represented boundary rather than the discovery mechanism. Tommy adjudicates the verdict from the paired evidence (manifests + transcript ordering + task correctness, compared against pre-change baseline runs of the same representative tasks). Falsification triggers rework of the element — revisit the contract, the map content, or the measurement — never silent continuation and never project abandonment.

#### B4. Deterministic context queries (NOT in this cut; earned increment)

Once the map and projections exist, agents should be able to *ask* for context rather than hunting through documents link by link. The design is **one parameterized query**, not four: a fixed procedure that takes a known anchor (a capability, a spine step, a policy) and a connection-type filter, mechanically follows the map's typed connections, and returns a bounded working set. The four early uses — orient to a capability, find governing policy, recover prerequisites, find verification surfaces — are named presets of that one procedure, differing only in their filters, not four surfaces to build and keep coherent.

Every answer shows its work: which connections were followed, which source backs each item, what was excluded and why, and any broken links encountered. Anchor resolution follows B0.1: a vague request is resolved into an exact anchor *before* the query, and both are recorded; the query itself runs mechanically. No general query language and no graph database until observed pressure earns them.

**Earning trigger:** B4 is built only when B3's evidence shows both that map-first orientation works (the map finds seams) and that agents demonstrably need multi-hop context assembly beyond what the B2/B3 projections already deliver — i.e., manifests/transcripts show agents chaining map lookups by hand. Until then it is design direction, not work.

## Assumptions

The load-bearing assumptions this design rests on, enumerated so the Confirmation block's "exercised vs. accepted untested" lines draw from something real:

1. **Run artifacts carry sufficient state** for a kernel-plus-fragments agent to work correctly from them. Untested; gated — the B2 role-competence test must exercise it before the break proceeds.
2. **The spine is the right selector** for context loading in spine-driven work. Grounded in current engine behavior; generalization to non-spine frames is explicitly deferred.
3. **An LLM can reliably find rhyming episode clusters** in a durable store. Untested; the collation exercise required by "done" is its first test.
4. **Manifests plus run transcripts suffice for map-use observability** without new watching infrastructure. Untested; B3's measurement is its first test, with a priced access trace as the named fallback.
5. **Deterministic recipes can express real working sets** — useful task context does not inherently require stochastic assembly. Partially grounded (the spine's gate notes already work this way); exercised by B2/B3.
6. **Every catastrophic-class invariant in the current corpus can be mechanized.** Untested; the invariant inventory below is the check, and discovering a third bin would be a design change requiring rework of B0.3.

## Testing pathways

These pathways are first exercises, not proofs: several mechanisms make aggregate claims (standing pressure against growth, rhymes findable across runs), and one instance cannot falsify an aggregate — evidence accumulates over repeated instances, with Tommy as the standing adjudicator of every verdict. Falsification of any pathway triggers rework of that element (contract, content, or measurement), never silent continuation and never project abandonment.

- **Map-first behavior (B3):** exercised by representative Commander tasks on the chosen dogfood corpus, with baseline runs of the same tasks captured before the contract change as the comparison arm. Evidence pairs the manifest (what was available, at which revision) with run-transcript ordering (map reads vs. source reads). Falsified if seams are consistently found by code crawling with map anchors backfilled afterward, or if map-first runs regress correctness against baseline. Corpus choice is the first implementing decision.
- **Degraded mode and drift (B3/B2):** exercised by deliberately inducing the detectable failures — remove or break the canonical entrypoint (run must report degraded mode, not silently crawl code) and change canon after committing a projection (the drift check must fail loudly). Falsified if either failure passes silently.
- **Tripwired deletion (B1):** exercised by performing real simplifications — removing prose doctrine with a filed predictive lesson — and running the affected workflows, accumulating instances over time rather than concluding from one. A worse-than-predicted impact is not failure: predictions are best-effort; reworking from the pre-attributed evidence is the mechanism working. Falsified only if the mechanism itself fails — a regression traceable to a deletion occurs and the tripwire never surfaces it or never connects it back. Corpus-size and per-role-surface trends from git history are the standing aggregate measure of whether deletion pressure works; they cost nothing to compute.
- **Projection determinism (B2):** exercised by rebuilding a projection from a clean checkout in a second environment (not just twice back-to-back — filesystem ordering, locale, and line endings are the real irreproducibility sources, and this corpus is developed on Windows), with a declared exclusion set for legitimately varying fields (timestamps, run ids) kept separate from content; and by making one doctrine change and confirming the projection diff is reviewable and complete. Falsified by irreproducible content or by drift the check misses.
- **Episode capture (B1):** exercised with a negative control: a run where the agent makes no effort to record anything must still yield the mechanical field group, and a run that hits a refusal/reopen must carry those events. Falsified if any mechanically-designated field depends on agent diligence. Cross-run retrieval gets its own exercise: seed episodes across several runs, consolidate one cluster, and confirm the store still finds rhymes involving consolidated episodes' neighbors.
- **Coherence sweep (B1):** exercised by seeding known incoherences into a bounded corpus slice and dispatching the adversarial panel; measured on how many seeded defects surface and at what noise ratio. Falsified if seeded defects are systematically missed, if findings mutate doctrine directly, or if they arrive unattributable.
- **Two-bin routing and invariant inventory (B0):** each consolidated cluster states its bin and, for prose, its tripwire — and the routing decision itself is reviewable evidence, not mere paperwork. The inventory pass over existing catastrophic-class invariants (confirming each is mechanism-owned or naming the gap) is a **named early implementing task of this cut with Tommy as owner**, not a deferred drill; assumption 6 stays open until it runs.
- **Confirm-gate refusal:** exercised once, cheaply — present `verify_spec_confirmed.py` with a partially-filled Confirmation block and an empty Disposition cell and confirm it refuses both. A gate claimed as a backstop should be seen refusing.

## Out of scope

- Federation across project maps and the cross-project idea commons — Stratum A; revisit after local map use is proven.
- A general query language (Cypher-like or otherwise) — one parameterized recipe with named presets only.
- The idea-substrate half of the vision (ideas themselves acquiring structure) — expected later work; this cut builds the instruction-system half and must not foreclose the other.
- Ontology expansion of the Cartographer map — the audit found the ontology is not the weakness.
- ActiveGraph adoption in any form.
- Assertion-strength mechanics, cross-frame mutation rules, and home-frame implementation — preserved on the ideas board for a future exploration.
- Success/clean-use telemetry beyond mechanism byproducts — new watching infrastructure must earn its way in via a specific question.
