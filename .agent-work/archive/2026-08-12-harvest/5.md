**Surfaced by the constellation feedback harvest (2026-06-24).** Three small commander-spine / convention fixes (all f1Brainz singles, medium confidence).

- **`compact-step-agent-uninvokable`** — the COMMANDER_SPINE `compact` step invokes `/compact`, a user-level CLI the agent cannot run; every run skips it with reason (a permanent dead step + skip ceremony). Make the imperative explicitly conditional (run if the harness exposes it, else skip-with-reason is the sanctioned path), or drop the step where harness auto-compaction covers context. _(issue-363)_

- **`reasoning-mission-crew-gate-mismatch`** — the execute gate mandates implementer/reviewer crew dispatch for every gate, even pure design-note/diagnosis gates where the Commander already holds the context and the deliverable is a document; a crew would be shallower. Add a "reasoning gate" mode that waives crew dispatch with stated reason and drives the gate in commander context. _(issue-394)_

- **`crew-survey-state-in-worktree-root`** — a crew reviewer's survey-checklist state (`.agent-work/g1-review/review.json`) was created at the worktree ROOT, leaving orphan untracked scratch the closeout had to identify and remove. Crew survey-checklists should be created under the issue workbench (`.agent-work/<work-id>/`) by convention/default. _(issue-447)_
