**Surfaced by the constellation feedback harvest (2026-06-24).** `constellation-commander` is a human-facing scaffold but is routinely driven autonomously under an Admiral, with no sanctioned mode for it — and an idle Agent-tool commander can drop its verdict.

### Occurrences
- **story_time** `commander-spine-mismatch-for-autonomous-dispatch` — the commander's `understand` interrogator "must reach the human", it pauses for `user-decision` checkpoints, and it launches nested `run_crew.py` crews. None fits an autonomous subagent under a run-to-completion Admiral on a harness where subagents cannot reach the human; the Admiral right-sized each Commander to a plan-driven single-agent implementer (re-derived per run).
- **story_time** `admiral-verifies-from-artifacts-on-commander-idle` — an Agent-tool commander sometimes ends with only an `idle_notification` (`idleReason: available`) and never delivers its verdict text, even when told to. Hit 2 of 4 reporting commanders; work was complete (artifacts were ground truth) but a future Admiral could hang waiting.
- **f1Brainz** `delegated-context-user-decision-checkpoints` — under an Admiral wave where the human pre-froze scope, the user-decision postconditions are correctly satisfied by citing the frozen launch order, but the skill text has no documented reading for the delegated case.

### Proposed fix
Document a sanctioned **autonomous commander mode** in the commander skill (or Admiral dispatch guidance): when to right-size to a plan-driven single-agent implementer, and that user-decision checkpoints are satisfied by citing a pre-frozen launch order with the human ratifying at the return boundary. Codify Admiral doctrine: **verify from the artifact set (branch/commit/PR/files) + clean-room on commander idle; never block on a verdict message.**
