## The load-bearing design call

σ⁺_c = s_c · σ_session, with s_c the class time share. Grip is a *session-level common-mode* effect (its PK is the session), so under perfect correlation the per-part scales add **linearly**, giving the coherence identity Σ_c σ⁺_c = σ_session — mirroring the point-side identity that already holds. Given normalised weights, linear is the unique law satisfying it. The critic pass caught that this identity holds on the **scale parameter of the underlying un-folded Student-t**, not on folded expectations, which is why the width-shape test asserts on `sigma_plus` and never on `upper_bound()` differences.

Two findings that shrank the work: the store needs **no schema change** (the row PK already includes `class`), and `fingerprint/fit.py` needs **no arithmetic change** (its cell grain is already `(driver, class)`).
