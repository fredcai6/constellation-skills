## Intent

Reformulate the physics→prediction **payload** as a decomposition. The physics program reassembles its measured capability envelopes into per-car **reference laps** (the modeled car's ideal), and measures each driver as **utilization of their own reference**, conditioned on the circuit's composition. Car = reference; driver = utilization. Through Builds 1–3 this changes **what** enters the predictor — decomposed, uncertainty-carrying quantities instead of raw entangled signals — while the injection *mechanism* (the toggle-gated fusion seam) is deliberately unchanged; full replacement of the classification-history truth channel (retraining the neural modules on decomposed inputs) is the roadmap endpoint (§8 tail), not a Builds-1–3 deliverable. (Reworded per review I1.)

For whom: the physics program's measurement layer first (Build 1's product is stores + instruments), the live sampled predictor at Build 3 (through the critical-eye feature family — payoff deferred *by design* until features prove reliable, per review I3), and the fantasy decision layer only as downstream program context, not a designed-for consumer of this spec (I5). Done feels like: Build 1 running end-to-end on 2023 — per-weekend segment maps, a grip baseline, a season-scale class-grain utilization store, fitted fingerprints, the join producing **quali-side** weekend priors (I6) — with the instrument panel reporting the *size* of every signal. Steering is by **allocation, not gating** (I2): §7's outputs carry named decisions — variance shares direct where Build-2 effort goes, per-class (and per-channel) replication decides which fingerprint axes earn join weight, sector calibration decides whether reference-lap fidelity work precedes fingerprint work. Sizing informs the route; it never halts the program.

Kill condition: **none at the frame level, by owner ruling.** Fingerprint non-replication is a diagnostic that routes to more structural work (different observables, different conditioning), never abandonment. The connective pipeline is a durable asset regardless — this exploration found five prior lineages of this same idea, unjoined and part-forgotten; ending that rediscovery loop is itself a goal.

## Standing design principles (owner-ratified, govern every section)

1. **Lowest dimensionality that solves the problem** — escalation layers exist in schemas from day one, activate on demand.
2. **No baked-in normality** — Student-t / heavy-tailed forms wherever possible.
3. **Segment draws are correlated, not independent** — correlation measured, not assumed; energy-carry is a physical coupling mechanism.
4. **No frame-kill** — nulls route to structural work; the pipeline is a durable asset.
5. **Critical eye on every feature** — physics enters prediction as a family of candidate features; NN reliance on them is the success signal.

### §1 Segment vocabulary & SegmentMap (load-bearing — design-it-twice in progress)

- **Canonical gate (owner-ruled):** corner segment = field-reference-lap lateral acceleration (v_ref² × curvature) above threshold; braking zone = field-envelope brake onset (robust low quantile p5–p10, never mean — the median-ribbon failure is the precedent) → corner entry; straight = remainder via the straight-arc grouper. Per-lap kinematic gates are demoted to observation filters; the segment map is fixed per weekend and laps are scored against it (driver-invariance by construction).
- **Severity ladder:** per-rules-era mixture over reference-lap (log-radius, lateral-g) descriptors — Student-t components where feasible, BIC-selected k capped small, held-out-circuit stability gate (F12) as per-era acceptance. Era pooling IS the cross-circuit class identity. Memberships stay SOFT (fractional weights flow through all consumers).
- **Nesting:** FIA sector lines derived per weekend by time-to-distance interpolation (pooled median, sub-meter); lines are mandatory cut points — straddling segments SPLIT into same-class pieces, never snapped.
- **Lifecycle:** seed-then-supersede. Live weekends seed from prior-year map + FIA event documentation; superseded the moment weekend data contradicts; reprocess-over-change-detection; every consumer records `map_version`. No cross-year corner-history; class-membership-only for the join (corner identity = arc-length within layout version, only for the dormant discriminativeness layer).
- **Schema carries dormant attributes:** sub-phase marks (entry/apex/exit), adjacency (neighbor class, following-straight length, direction flip), turn direction.
- **Interface — design-it-twice outcome (3 candidates: minimal / max-flexibility / common-caller-first; full texts in `excursions/D2-segmap-{min,flex,caller}-RESULT.md`). Recommendation = a three-way hybrid, each candidate winning the layer it was strongest on:**
  - **Runtime representation from the CALLER candidate:** SegmentMap is flat parallel numpy arrays (boundaries, lengths, int8 type/sector/direction codes, corner descriptors, zero-filled severity matrix), never a list of objects. Hot masking = one `searchsorted` (`segment_of(distances)`, wrap-modulo on the closed loop — out-of-range never raises); the MC race sim fetches three read-only arrays once, zero per-draw cost. `reclassify_severity(mixture)` decouples geometry from classification: a mixture refit is an O(n_corners) membership recompute, never a geometry rebuild, and vice versa.
  - **Identity & persistence semantics from the FLEX candidate:** *labels, never positions.* Class memberships persist as tidy rows keyed by `vocabulary_id`/`class_id` strings minted once at fit time (`VocabularyRef` registry; era refits and k-changes are pure inserts, no migration, no index-shift corruption). Version axes are orthogonal: map_version (seed-then-supersede chain per weekend), layout identity (content-hash of geometry-only cut points, deliberately excluding sector-forced splits), schema/format version, vocabulary fit_version. At load, labeled memberships materialize into the caller-shaped positional matrix plus a `class_ids` column map — persistence is label-safe, the hot path is positional, the load boundary converts exactly once.
  - **Lifecycle from the MINIMAL candidate:** one deep write entry point carrying the whole cold/seeded/superseded lifecycle as a single compare-and-branch (no partial-lifecycle calls, coherent provenance always; inconclusive contradiction ⇒ keep the seed); three narrow reads with deliberately distinct miss-contracts (current/by-version raise loudly, latest-for-seeding returns None on genuine cold start); no update/patch surface anywhere; supersede requires a recorded contradiction reason; provenance carries config fingerprint + FIA doc citations.
  - **Convergent across all three (adopted without debate):** adjacency computed, never persisted; sub-phases marks-only and dormant (`resolution="subphase"` reserved in signature, backing store deferred); sector-nesting fails CLOSED (`SectorLineUnavailableError`) rather than emitting a map without the invariant; min-segment-length sliver merging exempts sector cuts (split-not-snap preserved exactly); no aggregate-statistics methods on the map (rollups are consumer-side); no grip state in the map (grip mutates session-by-session, the map versions weekend-by-weekend — consumer 6 pays one join so consumers 1/4 get an immutable map); severity mixture consumed as a fitted input behind a Protocol (Student-t swap doesn't touch SegmentMap).
  - **Build-1 phasing (review S3/S6) + frozen constants (T4):** Build 1 implements the cold/historical derivation path only — the seeded/supersede branch of the deep entry point ships with Build 3, when live weekends exist; the interface is unchanged now. Version axes exercised in Build 1: weekend key, layout identity, vocabulary fit version (the per-weekend supersede chain is trivially v1 in batch mode; format version lives in the store convention). The braking-onset quantile and corner lateral-g threshold are **frozen as named constants chosen before the first real-data map derivation** (F12 discipline); changing them afterward requires a new named constant set and full re-derivation, never silent tuning.

### §2 Grip baseline module G

- One canonical module owns per-weekend track grip state; every consumer subtracts identically (ends the half-implementation scatter).
- Intra-session: saturating curve in **cumulative car-laps** (not wall clock). Inter-session: free offset per session start, estimated from data (absorbs overnight/support-series/temperature — support laps are invisible to our data by construction and land in the offset). Rain flag forces offset re-estimation with wide σ.

- **Validation gate (self-scoring):** same driver/compound/similar-fuel reconciles across sessions after subtracting G; before/after reconciliation error is the module's per-weekend scorecard.
- Escalation (dormant): per-class evolution curves.
- Interface: follows the estimate-store pattern (fit artifact + query surface); design-it-twice skipped — precedented shape, stated reason.

### §4 DriverFingerprint: store, fit, and the join (load-bearing — design-it-twice in progress)

- Per (driver, rules-era): class-level utilization cells (mean, σ, support n), fit via hierarchical Student-t shrinkage (field mean → driver-overall → class cell, + class-across-drivers parent), recency-weighted. **Slow-offline-loop mutation only** — fingerprints never move during a weekend.
- **The join (the core product):** circuit per-class time-share vector × cells, soft-membership-weighted → expected weekend utilization prior with honest σ (thin cells → fat σ). The linear join IS the prior; escalation to sequence/bespoke formulations only if arithmetic under-explains.
- Race side (Build 2, interface designed now): push + managed Student-t distributions per class + consistency (variance) channel.
- **Interface — design-it-twice outcome (3 candidates: minimal / max-flexibility / common-caller-first; full texts in `excursions/D2-fp-{min,flex,caller}-RESULT.md`). Recommendation = three-way hybrid:**
  - **Statistical core & conventions from the CALLER candidate — reuse over invention, verified before trusted (review T9):** the hierarchy "field mean → driver-overall → class cell + class-across-drivers parent" maps onto the EXISTING `fit_two_way` pooling primitive with axes reinterpreted as driver×class — adopted as the **candidate** primitive, contingent on a synthetic-recovery validation under realistic driver×class imbalance (its method-of-moments components are exact only for balanced grids, per its own docstring, and driver×class support is decidedly unbalanced); the named fallback is #628-style direct per-(driver, class) pooling with explicit parent shrinkage. Either way, thin-support→fat-tail behavior via the repo's canonical Student-t seam (`predictive_t`, project-wide ν defaults) — no *invented* statistical machinery in Build 1 (a true t-shrinkage upgrade stays possible behind the same interface). Units are **absolute deficit (m/s), never a ratio** — adopting #628's anti-circularity-hardened convention (the min candidate's ratio proposal is REJECTED with that reason). The fingerprint vocabulary is **corner-severity only; straights are excluded** per the existing confounded-negative-control ruling — composition vectors sum to the corner share, not 1.0, and carry an explicit distance-vs-time-share provenance flag. Rules-era key = the existing `RegulationEra.for_season` seam. Race side: dense (driver×class×{push,managed}) array table built once per race, vectorized Student-t sampling, with `push_managed_split` as a fully swappable config object (P6's finding — the split is *constructed*, not discovered — is baked into the seam placement). Every fingerprint is always fully populated: k cells exactly, `unresolved` status rather than missing rows.
  - **Address space & versioning from the FLEX candidate (trimmed per review S1/S5/S10):** a versioned `CellAddress` (era, class-vocabulary version, class id) plus the dormant `channel` dimension (`"utilization"` today, `"energy"` when activated — see §7's pre-registered comparison) and *what*-measures as slot strings (push/managed/consistency/management-efficiency) — escalations are inserts, never migrations; segment-discriminativeness lives in fit-time observable weighting, zero address cost. **Sub-phase and transition dimensions are single-homed in SegmentMap and deliberately NOT duplicated here** — if those layers activate, fingerprint addressing derives them from the map's vocabulary then (S5); the finer-cell regeneration-discipline guarantee is dropped with them (S10 — no named trigger exists). `ClassVocabulary` carries the F12 stability verdict, and fitting against a failed-gate vocabulary is refused by default (explicit override only). Tidy persistence with a canonical non-NULL `cell_key` (SQLite NULL-PK gotcha designed around). The factorization-compatibility clause is deleted (S1 — the approach is shelved; the read boundary gets redesigned if it revives).
  - **Discipline from the MINIMAL candidate:** `as_of_round` is REQUIRED with no default — the strictly-pre causal cutoff applied to the driver side, citing #628's measured 14.6×-materiality leakage precedent; full-era retrospectives pass a cutoff past the era's end (there is no "no cutoff" mode). Thinness is priced ONCE at fit time (σ widening); the join propagates honestly and never re-filters — thin exposure surfaced via `thin_classes`/`weight_on_thin` fields, not silent discounting. Era and vocabulary mismatches refuse loudly (no silent substitution); mutation is structurally slow-loop-only (no fit-on-read path exists); persistence is replace-on-rerun.
  - **Convergent across all three (adopted):** soft class memberships flow through fit and join; every cross-boundary call pins the class-vocabulary version; the join is reserved for the practice update and fusion summaries while the race simulator and instrument panel read un-aggregated cells directly; evo reaches this module only through a thin config-gated adapter (the `physics_feature_injection` pattern) — physics never imports evo.

### §7 Instrument panel (Build 1's exit gate — sizing, never go/no-go; outputs carry the named allocation decisions in Intent)

- **Variance decomposition:** segment-time variance → car-reference / driver-utilization / residual shares (driver share reads as a floor). THE "set the size" instrument; directs Build-2 effort.
- **Residual split-half replication:** per-class fingerprint replication AFTER removing overall skill (the golf-corrected form) — raw replication flatters by smuggling overall skill back in. Doubles as σ-honesty check (cells replicate within stated uncertainty). Thresholds and the support-count-scaling formula frozen pre-run (review T6).
- **Pre-registered channel comparison (owner-initiated, 2026-07-25):** fingerprint cells are fit in BOTH channels — time-deficit (`"utilization"`) and energy (`"energy"`: kinetic-energy deficit derived from the same speed observables) — and residual split-half replication runs per channel per class. Whichever channel replicates better in a class earns join weight there. Registered before any real-data run, alongside the frozen replication constants. Rationale: time weights segments by lap-time cost (driver-signal-aligned); energy natively expresses momentum-carry and deployment (car/2026-aligned) — the comparison decides empirically instead of on priors.
- **Composed-sector scorecard:** segment predictions sum exactly into FIA sectors; validated against official sector times (already on disk) every pipeline weekend — central values AND distribution calibration coverage, with the coverage criterion pre-registered (review T11).

## Testing pathways

Each pathway is tagged **[GATING]** (correctness — failure blocks the build until fixed) or **[DIAGNOSTIC]** (sizing — informs allocation, never halts; owner no-kill ruling). This tagging resolves review T8: correctness bugs gate, signal sizes never do. All thresholds/constants below are **frozen before the first real-data run** (F12 discipline); post-hoc changes require a new named constant set and a re-run.

- **SegmentMap [GATING]:** tiling completeness and sector-nesting exactness are *construction checks* — honest label per review T3: they catch coverage and arithmetic bugs, not mis-typing. The substantive checks: cross-weekend map stability (boundary drift between same-circuit weekends under a frozen threshold) and typing spot-checks against independently-derived references (the ephemeris pilot's Bahrain corner windows; published corner inventories). Falsified by unstable or physically wrong maps, not just gaps.
- **Grip module G [GATING]:** **held-out** reconciliation (review T1) — G fit on a subset of drivers/laps, scored on the disjoint remainder, never in-sample; plus a synthetic-recovery test (known ground-truth curve + session offsets injected into simulated pace must be separably recovered — review T2's identifiability risk). Falsified if held-out reconciliation fails to improve or synthetic recovery fails.
- **Class ladder [inherited in Build 1; GATING at backfill]:** Build 1 consumes the existing validated vocabulary (#638, PASS 5/5); the per-era refit + F12 gate run when backfill provides the circuit diversity the gate needs (review T10 — open deferral, not silent).
- **Utilization observables [GATING]:** deficits-sum-to-lap is a construction check (review T5); the substantive check is attribution robustness — per-class deficits stable under boundary jitter within the frozen quantile's uncertainty (jackknife over derivation laps).
- **Join arithmetic [GATING]:** reduces-to-simple-case unit invariants (review T7) — uniform composition returns the driver-overall mean; identical cells return that constant regardless of composition; σ propagation collapses correctly for a single-class circuit. A mechanically broken join cannot hide behind outcome-level wins.
- **Fingerprint replication [DIAGNOSTIC]:** residual split-half replication after removing overall skill, per class **and per channel** (time and energy — §7's pre-registered comparison), support-count-scaled expectation formula and thresholds frozen pre-run (review T6). Output allocates join weight; weak replication routes to structural work.
- **Join prior quality [DIAGNOSTIC]:** held-out-weekend comparison — fingerprint×composition vs driver-overall-only prior (the golf null is the benchmark to beat). A sizing measurement; the correctness of the join is established by the GATING unit invariants above, not by this.
- **Composed sectors [split per review T11]:** position-sum exactness = construction check; the genuine external test is predicted sector-time distribution calibration against official FIA times, coverage criterion pre-registered — [DIAGNOSTIC] for signal size, [GATING] on a frozen gross-miscalibration sanity bound.
- **Deferred to later drills:** push/managed classifier validation (Build 2, post-facto quali anchor); practice-update calibration (Build 3); fusion A/B (the original value question, now fed by a decomposed payload).
