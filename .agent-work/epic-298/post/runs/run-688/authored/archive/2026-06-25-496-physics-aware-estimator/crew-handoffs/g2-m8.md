# G2 Spike M8 — Semi-parametric onset mean function

(Read `g2-COMMON.md` in the same dir first — shared context, scoreboard seam, output rules.)

## Thesis
A smoothness prior cannot bend a sharp step without either rounding it or ringing. M8 removes the
step from what the smoother must bend: model the brake-onset with a PARAMETRIC mean function (a
change-point / ramp / sigmoid keyed to brake-apply time), and let the smoother fit only the smooth
RESIDUAL around it. The sharp step lives in the mean; the GP/Kalman never has to represent it.

## Concrete approach
- Detect each braking onset (regime → `"straight_brake"`, or `|dv/dt|` threshold). For each, fit a
  parametric onset shape to the raw longitudinal signal — e.g. a sigmoid/logistic or piecewise-linear
  ramp in `a_long` (or a corresponding speed ramp) with a few parameters (onset time, depth, width),
  fit to `inp.a_long_raw` (RAW only — never the smoothed trajectory). This is the "mean function"
  `m(t)`: sharp where the onset is, flat elsewhere.
- Decompose: form a residual signal by removing the parametric onset from the observations, smooth
  the residual with the existing Gaussian smoother (`inp.make_smoother(nu_proc=None)` path), then
  ADD the parametric mean back. Net `a_long = m(t) + smoothed_residual(t)`. Decide the cleanest space
  to do this (you may operate in speed space and differentiate, or directly compose `a_long`); state
  your choice and why it is consistent.
- Your `VariantFn` returns the composed `a_long`.

## Distinction from M1/M7 (so the portfolio stays informative)
M1 anchors a PHYSICS-MODEL value; M7 anchors a DENOISED-RAW value; M8 uses a PARAMETRIC FUNCTIONAL
FORM (a generic change-point/ramp) as a mean and smooths the residual — it does not assume the
`a_b+b_b·v²` physics, just a sharp-step basis. Keep your prototype true to this "mean-function
decomposition" framing.

## Invariant note
The mean is fit from RAW only (external & un-biased of the smoothed trajectory). It does not use the
kind=3 anchor channel; it is a mean-subtraction decomposition. State how it stays within the spirit
of `decision:two_cycle_external_anchor_design` (raw-only) even though it is structurally different.

## Watch for
- The parametric form must be flexible enough to fit real onsets but not so flexible it absorbs real
  variation. Report how many onsets fit cleanly vs poorly per circuit.
- Bahrain is the target (a deep sharp onset is exactly what a ramp mean captures). Monaco: confirm
  the residual-smooth does not ring (the mean should absorb the transient that was causing ringing).
