# Research Handoff: Recursive Agent Learning Without Instruction Accretion

## One named question

How do active agent-learning and self-improvement systems convert experience into reusable capability without appending every lesson to the runtime instruction context?

## Context to preserve

- Constellation is a recursively learning agent workflow system.
- It captures lessons, feedback, doctrine, templates, and mechanical rails, but repeated improvements are producing instruction density and scattered interfaces.
- The human wants to remain involved in hard reasoning; the goal is not unsupervised self-modification.
- This is a personal experimentation project. Concepts may be borrowed, but no external system will be reused directly.
- Keep the analysis at the support-system and lifecycle level. Do not design individual truth-update behaviors.

## Required comparison

Select 5-8 genuinely distinct systems or research lines. Plausible families include reflective verbal learning, evolving skill libraries, prompt/program optimization, memory systems, context management, policy distillation, and continual-learning evaluation.

For each distinguish:

1. What experience becomes durable.
2. Whether learning appends, retrieves, compiles, replaces, distills, or prunes.
3. What enters runtime context and when.
4. How conflicting, stale, redundant, or harmful lessons are handled.
5. Whether the system measures regression or global corpus quality.
6. Who or what has authority to promote the learned artifact.
7. What mechanism transfers to Constellation and what trap should be avoided.

## Constraints

- Use current primary papers, official repositories, and official documentation as of 2026-07-30.
- Do not install packages, run experiments, modify source, or recommend adoption.
- Distinguish sourced facts from inference.
- Treat self-reported benchmarks as scoped claims.
- Report tested and NOT tested scope.
