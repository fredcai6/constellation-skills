## What the investigation changed about the issue

**The issue's premise is misattributed, and I'd flag this to you first.** There is no rain exclusion in `grip_baseline.py`. `run_grip_batch` stores *every* session; `rain_flag` is used at exactly two places and both only multiply `session_offset_sigma` by a flat 4.0. The 20-of-36 weekend drop happened inside the #678 spike's own candidate selection (`scratchpad/g-pool-spike/`, never committed). The defect is real but sits one level up: **no production selection predicate exists**, so every consumer invents one — and the only universally-available signal to invent it from is the any-wet bool.

**The acceptance criteria's first-named remedy is measurably the wrong instrument.** I measured 2022–2024 (`probe_rain.py`, read-only). A fraction-of-wet-*samples* threshold fails in both directions:
- 2023 Belgium Q — 1 wet sample of 95 (1.1%), but **212 of 341 laps on wet tyres**. A sample-fraction rule *keeps* a soaked qualifying session.
- 2022 Hungary R — 46 of 167 samples wet (27.5%), **zero** wet laps. Still dropped. Same at Miami R, Great Britain R, Austria R, and 2024 Hungary Q (76% of samples wet, not one wet lap).

The discriminating instrument is the WET/INTERMEDIATE **lap** fraction — which this repo already uses under a named threshold in the fuel subsystem (`burn_rate_calibration.WET_EXCLUDE_THRESHOLD`), map-sanctioned as a `physics → data` read. But it's populated for **races only**: zero non-race rows in every season 2022–2025. That's what makes the data-layer coverage gate load-bearing rather than optional.

**One hazard the issue's acceptance implies but doesn't name:** the fit already hard-filters to dry compounds, so wet laps never enter it. The real exposure is that a recovered mixed session's *surviving dry laps sit on a drying track* — falling lap times from drying, not rubber accumulation, which violates the monotone model directly. Hence a drying-window guard as its own gate.

The adversarial pass caught one thing I'd nearly shipped: the re-batch uses `force=True`, which **overwrites the grip store in place** — destroying the "before" half of the held-out comparison the plan itself requires. A pre-re-batch snapshot is now mandatory.

**Decisions I made in your absence, flagged loudest first:** grading the flat 4× sigma inflation (a scope extension that changes stored sigmas — isolated in gate g4 so it drops whole if you disagree); including the drying-window guard; owning the data-layer coverage extension; shipping a predicate with no live consumer yet.

Two rigor mechanisms ran in reduced form — the session's no-subagent directive meant the plan-alternatives panel was authored sequentially rather than in parallel, and no genuinely *cold* critic could read the plan. That's a real reduction; the owed cold read is wired into the plan as `e0-context.c2` and logged as triage so it can't be lost.
